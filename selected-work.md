# Selected work

## Burgut Cargo

Warehouse staff record a shipment’s status once. Customers see that update on the tracking page or receive it through Telegram. The Django backend also handles trucks, tariffs, and Excel imports.

[Source on GitHub](https://github.com/jamshid-muxsinov/burgut-cargo)

## Logistics Sim

Students work through a shipment scenario and choose how to respond to delays, customs holds, or a change of route. The instructor can follow the session as decisions come in.

[Source on GitHub](https://github.com/jamshid-muxsinov/sim-sorex)

## Exam Forge

Students import their own TXT question bank and practise offline in a Flutter app. Missed questions go into a review set and leave it after three consecutive correct answers.

## задача.

Teams submit and review work inside a Telegram mini app. A manager can accept a result or return it with a comment, and the notification takes the assignee back to the task.

## Generator Store

Customers browse industrial equipment in Russian or Uzbek and send their cart as an inquiry. A sales manager can claim it directly in Telegram.

## Team lunches

Employees confirm whether they’re coming to lunch. Kitchen staff get a portion count before the meal and can read feedback afterwards.

## Бирка

Staff count inventory at each location. Managers compare the count with the records and can send discrepancies back for another check.
