# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Technical collaborators, potential clients or employers, and other builders evaluating Jamshid Muxsinov through his GitHub profile. They need to understand the breadth of the work, see credible product evidence, and find the projects most relevant to them without reading a generic developer biography.

## Product Purpose

Present Jamshid as a product-oriented technical builder who works across backend systems, automation, AI tooling, mobile, infrastructure, and interface design. Success means the profile communicates a coherent personal point of view while giving the full body of work enough space to remain varied and specific.

## Brand Personality

Controlled, restless, and product-minded. The voice is direct and concrete. The emotional goal is curiosity grounded by confidence: many things are being built, but the person building them understands the systems underneath.

## Anti-references

Generic developer portfolios, AI or SaaS landing pages, cyberpunk terminals, neon hacker imagery, industrial brutalism, engineering dossiers, declassified blueprints, system-atlas decoration, beige editorial boards, Swiss and terminal hybrids, fake technical labels, thin-rule grids, oversized grotesk paired with tiny monospace metadata, pseudo-intellectual microcopy, and repetitive project-card layouts.

## Design Principles

1. Let real project material carry the identity.
2. Show breadth without turning every project into the same module.
3. Keep the underlying structure controlled while allowing selective overlap and asymmetry.
4. Use plain language and concrete mechanisms instead of portfolio slogans.
5. Keep projects without strong visual evidence honest and visible through native Markdown.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep essential information in searchable Markdown, supply meaningful alt text for every composition, preserve legibility at desktop GitHub width and 360px mobile width, avoid motion-dependent meaning, and maintain sufficient contrast within all raster assets.
