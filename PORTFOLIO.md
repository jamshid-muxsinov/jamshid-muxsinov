## Selection logic

- **Primary** combines distinctive mechanism, product breadth, credible implementation evidence, and useful portfolio contrast.
- **Secondary** is real working material that deepens the system map without competing for the opening screen.
- **Lab** remains visible as evidence of curiosity and iteration, but does not borrow maturity claims from stronger projects.
- Superseded duplicates are represented once in the public profile and retained here only to preserve the development lineage.
